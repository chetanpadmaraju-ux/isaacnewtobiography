About Isaac Newton

(4 January 1643 – 31 March 1727) was an English polymath, physicist, mathematician, and astronomer who laid the foundation for classical mechanics.

Early life and Education

Sir Isaac Newton was born in the 4th January 1643 in Woolsthorpe, Lincolnshire, England, shortly after his father's death.He enterd Trinity college in cambridge in 1661 earning his Bachelors degree in 1665

His early breakthrough discoveries

Published Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica (1687), formulating the three laws of motion and the law of universal gravitation.Aswell as that ,he Proved that white light is a mix of colored rays using a prism and built the first practical reflecting telescope.
